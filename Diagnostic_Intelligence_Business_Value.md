## Executive Summary

**The Business Case:** Diagnostic Intelligence (DI) transforms ALLDATA from a **content library** into an **intelligent diagnostic partner**, creating a defensible competitive moat through AI while generating new revenue streams and improving customer retention.

**Investment Justification:** DI addresses critical market shifts:
1. **Technician shortage crisis** - 642,000 technician shortage by 2024 (TechForce Foundation)
2. **Vehicle complexity explosion** - Modern vehicles have 100M+ lines of code
3. **First-time fix pressure** - Shops lose $500-$1,500 per misdiagnosis
4. **Competitive threat** - Mitchell 1, Identifix, and new AI entrants

**Projected Impact:**
- **$15-25M** incremental annual revenue (add-on subscriptions)
- **10-15%** improvement in customer retention
- **25-40%** increase in average revenue per user (ARPU)
- **30%+** reduction in customer churn risk
- **Market leadership** in AI-powered diagnostics
